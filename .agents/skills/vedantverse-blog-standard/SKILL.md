# VedantVerse Performance Marketing Editorial Blueprint

This skill defines the mandatory structure, voice, depth, and formatting for all articles published on **VedantVerse** (`vedantverse.in/blog`). Every blog post must strictly adhere to this standard.

---

## 1. Core Principles & Voice

1. **Author Persona**: Vedant Tiwari, Senior Performance Marketing Executive & Media Planner in Mumbai managing high-velocity paid media budgets across Meta Ads, Google Ads (Search, Shopping, Performance Max), GA4, CAPI, and programmatic platforms.
2. **Tone**: Direct, empirical, technical, authoritative, zero-fluff. Never sound like generic marketing fluff or AI-generated filler. Use concrete terms: *CPL, CPR, blended CAC, MER, first-party cookie lifetimes, Event Match Quality (EMQ), bid caps, cost caps, learning phase thresholds (50 conversions/week), CAPI deduplication*.
3. **Information Density**: Front-load value immediately. The reader must have their core answer and strategic rule within the first 60 seconds of reading.
4. **Real-World Evidence**: Every post must reference real-world operational execution, citing actual numbers, percentages, or client case studies (e.g., CURA, Cutistic, Bellissima, Pannest, Gujju Express).

---

## 2. Mandatory Structural Architecture

Every article MUST contain the following 14 sequential sections in order:

### 1. Title (H1)
* High-intent, search-optimized, 2026-relevant, and direct.
* Example: `# Meta Ads vs Google Ads for D2C Brands in India (2026): Which to Start With and How to Split Budget`

### 2. Byline
* Format: `By Vedant Tiwari, Senior Performance Marketer & Media Planner, Mumbai · Updated [Date] · [X] min read`

### 3. Executive "Short answer" (TL;DR)
* A bolded 2–4 sentence summary answering the question upfront with decisive guidance.
* Example: `**Short answer:** Start with Google Search if people already search for what you sell...`

### 4. Key Takeaways (`## Key takeaways`)
* 4 to 6 concise, bulleted rules of thumb capturing the core strategic insights of the article.

### 5. Core Comparative or Metric Table (`## [Topic] at a glance`)
* A high-density markdown table comparing platforms, campaign types, metrics, or frameworks.
* Columns should be clear, e.g., `| Lever | Meta Ads | Google Ads | Benchmark |`.

### 6. Strategic Decision Framework (`## How to decide [or diagnose]...`)
* 3 to 4 sequential, numbered decision questions or diagnostic criteria with clear rules of thumb.

### 7. Tactical Allocation / Budget Matrix (`## How to split budget [or structure campaigns]...`)
* A structured table providing actionable breakdowns by stage, AOV, or spend level.
* Must explain the *Why* behind each allocation.

### 8. Technical Prerequisites (`## Tracking comes before scale` or foundational architecture)
* Technical checklist covering CAPI, server-side tracking, GA4 + GTM, store backend reconciliation, and attribution deduplication.

### 9. Real-World Case Evidence (`## Real example: [Metric / Platform Scaling]`)
* Concrete client data (e.g., CURA's 46% CPR reduction from ₹278 to ₹149, or Cutistic's 4.60x ROAS).
* Bullets highlighting the operational levers pulled and a markdown hyperlink to the internal case study or article.

### 10. Costly Mistakes (`## Common mistakes that waste budget`)
* 4 to 6 specific operational mistakes to avoid (e.g., unconstrained PMax, changing multiple variables at once, starving Meta's 50-conversion learning phase).

### 11. Tactical Roadmap (`## A simple 30-60-90 day plan` or Sequential Execution Phases)
* Broken down into **Days 1–30: Foundations**, **Days 31–60: Learn and cut**, and **Days 61–90: Scale carefully**.

### 12. Frequently Asked Questions (`## Frequently asked questions`)
* 4 to 6 high-value questions formatted with `### Question` and 1–2 paragraph direct answers.
* Optimized for Google's `FAQPage` schema markup and AI Overview citations.

### 13. Work With Me (`## Work with me`)
* Direct call to action linking to consultation:
  `I run Meta Ads and Google Ads for D2C, travel, logistics and real estate brands. If you want a channel plan built around your margins and demand type, [get in touch](https://vedantverse.in/#contact).`

### 14. Author Bio (`## About the author`)
* Standard bio:
  `Vedant Tiwari is a Senior Performance Marketing Executive and Media Planner based in Mumbai. He plans, runs and reports on paid media across Meta Ads and Google Ads, and writes about what works at [VedantVerse](https://vedantverse.in).`

## 3. SEO Metadata Footer (Required for storage)

Every JSON/post record must include:
* **Title Tag**: 50–60 characters.
* **Meta Description**: 150–160 characters.
* **Slug**: Clean, keyword-rich kebab-case.
* **Category**: e.g., `Paid Media & Strategy`, `Tracking & Telemetry`, `E-Commerce & D2C`, `B2B & Lead Gen`.
* **Tags**: 6–10 focused search tags.
* **Schema**: Article, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList.
